2How to get value out of this

✅ Do
  • Read the explanation even when you were right — you may have been right for the wrong reason, which will not survive a rephrased stem
  • Read the per-option explanations. Knowing why B is wrong is what lets you eliminate B's cousin next time
  • Use Retry missed repeatedly until that list is empty
  • Drill a single domain while you are learning it; save full mocks for after you have covered everything
  • Take at least one mock in a single uninterrupted 180-minute sitting
🚫 Don't
  • Grind questions without reading explanations — that is measuring, not learning
  • Take mock after mock hoping the score drifts up. If a domain is under 72%, go back to the module
  • Memorise which letter is correct. The bank shuffles, and so does the real exam
  • Treat a good score on questions you have already seen as evidence of anything

A caveat about the scaled score
AWS reports 100–1000 with 750 to pass, but does not publish the raw-to-scaled mapping and applies psychometric scaling per exam form. The score here is a straightforward linear mapping of your percentage — useful as a consistent yardstick against itself, not a prediction of your official score. Treat 800 here as the safe target, not 750.

Your storage, your data
